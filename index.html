THE WEEKLY BOARD

A 4-week rotation built around two smaller shop-and-batch-cook sessions each week — Sunday and Wednesday — so nothing sits in the fridge too long. Each batch covers dinner the night it's cooked through the next round of lunches, Thursday through Saturday dinners are cooked fresh, and Sunday lunch is left open for eating out. Same targets and portions every week; the flavors rotate.

Batch Prep Schedule
Two smaller cooking sessions instead of one big one, so nothing sits in the fridge more than 3–4 days. Same rhythm every week — only the recipes change.
Grocery List
Split into two fresh-food runs so nothing spoils by week's end — plus a pantry list you can grab anytime.
Pantry Buy once — keeps all week
🛒 Trip 1 — Saturday Feeds Sunday dinner through Wednesday lunch
🛒 Trip 2 — Wednesday Feeds Wednesday dinner through Saturday (Sunday lunch is skipped — eating out)

Eggs, egg whites, Greek yogurt, kefir, milk, and frozen berries are only on Trip 1's list — they keep well past Wednesday, so there's no need to buy them twice. Everything else fresh (meat, fish, produce, cheese) is split across the two trips so it stays close to its purchase date. The pantry list (rice, oats, pasta, nuts, spices, protein powder) is shelf-stable — grab it whenever's convenient. Rice shows up in 3 recipes a week, so the 7–8 lb total is for the whole 4-week rotation, not one trip. A few items — fresh mozzarella, basil, asparagus/green beans, cherry tomatoes — are easy Whole Foods or Target add-ons if Aldi doesn't have them that week. Checked-off items are saved automatically.
